Our Story

Monument exists to create endurance events in places that deserve to be experienced.

Our journey begins with trail running on the Cotswold Edge, centred on North Nibley and the iconic Tyndale Monument. With its dramatic hills, ancient woodland and extensive network of trails, we believe this landscape provides the perfect setting to begin the Monument story and reflects everything we want the brand to stand for.

02

Our Vision

While the Cotswold Edge is where our story starts, it is not where our ambition ends.

Our long-term vision is to establish Monument as a trusted endurance events brand, creating exceptional experiences in remarkable locations across the UK. Every event will be shaped by its landscape, celebrate the character of its local community, and embody our commitment to thoughtful organisation, memorable participant experiences, and respect for the natural environment.

03

Our Principles

  • Thoughtful organisation.
  • Memorable participant experiences.
  • Respect for the natural environment.
  • A positive legacy.

04

Our Approach

We believe exceptional endurance events are created through thoughtful planning, collaboration and attention to detail. As Monument develops, we will continue working closely with experienced event organisers, local communities and governing bodies.
